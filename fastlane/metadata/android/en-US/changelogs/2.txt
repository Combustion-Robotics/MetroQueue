MetroQueue 1.1.0: Mix

Mix
- Crossfade between songs, with the next song pre-buffered and transitions starting where a song actually ends
- Transition styles: Fade, Filter, Echo, Rise and Cut, or Auto
- Smart mix: analyses tempo and structure to pick the transition point and style for each pair of songs
- Optional crossfade when skipping manually
- Custom transitions per song pair, saved and included in backups
- Full-screen transition editor: waveforms of both songs, drag to set the overlap and fades, snap to beats, pinch to zoom, style chips, match tempo, preview
- Replay a finished transition from the editor to fine-tune it
- Transition button between the current and next song in the queue
- "Transitions" in the Now Playing menu, next to Equalizer, with quick Mix settings

Voice
- Play songs with Gemini / Google Assistant voice requests

Fixes
- The next song's tempo now shows in the transition editor
- Clearing the app from recents during a transition no longer leaves the volume half-faded
