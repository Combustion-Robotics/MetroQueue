MetroQueue 1.4.0: Vibe mode

Vibe mode
- Full-screen, animated pixel-art scenes with your music: Winter dusk, Rainy city night, Starry lake and Spring blossoms
- Falling snow and petals, rain, fireflies, twinkling and shooting stars, drifting clouds, lightning, chimney smoke
- Each vibe plays its own endless radio, or keep what you're already listening to
- Create your own vibes: pick sky, scene, foreground and effects, and play a library playlist, a YouTube Music playlist (searchable) or an endless radio
- Small song bar with play/pause and next, adapting to portrait and landscape
- Open it from Now Playing ⋮ > Vibe mode; the Vibes tab can be turned on in Appearance
- Also on TV, from the side menu

TV
- Lyrics now use the free space on the right of Now Playing

Fixes
- The accent colour now follows every song change (also after crossfades)
- The app no longer shows an update when you're already on the latest version
- Dev pre-releases no longer appear in the in-app changelog

Defaults (only if you never changed them)
- Pure black (AMOLED) theme on
- Classic player design
