MetroQueue 1.3.0: Lyrics

Experimental lyrics
- Glow on every sung word, lined up with your lyrics font, fading out smoothly at the end of a line
- Soft halo behind the current line, which also grows slightly
- Depth blur and dimming on the surrounding lines
- Glow that follows the music (brightens with louder parts)
- Animated background in the artwork's colours, gently swelling on the beat
- Instrumental-break indicator now shows during real breaks and breathes with the beat
- New setting: Appearance > Lyrics > Lyrics effects (Full / Subtle / Off); battery saver switches to Subtle
- Smoother animations and much lower battery and CPU use: no full redraw every frame, effects pause while paused or when the player is minimised
- The screen only stays on while music is playing

Mix
- Manual skips blend in at most 1 s, and quick or repeated skips cut straight through with no overlapping audio
